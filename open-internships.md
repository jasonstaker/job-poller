**How this is ranked.** Urgency = relevance + recency.

- *Relevance* favours Summer 2027 first, then Fall 2027 / Winter 2028, then untermed
  co-op/intern roles. Core software engineering scores above ML/robotics-adjacent, and
  test/QA-focused roles are nudged down. Anything on the **2026** cycle, an intern
  conversion req, or a New Grad role without a 2027/2028 anchor is pushed to the bottom —
  those are for people graduating now, not April 2028.
- *Recency* is days since posted. Greenhouse reports `updated_at` rather than a true post
  date, so a few ages there may read fresher than the role really is. Workday reports
  prose ("Posted 4 Days Ago"), capped at 30. Six roles publish no date at all and are
  scored as if three weeks old, marked `?`.

Tuning is a one-line edit to the scoring if the weighting feels wrong.
